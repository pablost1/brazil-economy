What is this?

The unemployment rate is the percentage of the labor force (people working or actively looking for work) who do not have a job. People who gave up looking are not counted, which is why economists also watch 'underemployment' and informality.

Today the official number comes from IBGE's PNAD Contínua, a household survey covering the whole country, published as a rolling three-month average. Before 2012 there was no equivalent national survey; the older PME covered only six metropolitan regions, so the two lines are not exactly comparable, but they overlap from 2012 to 2016 so you can see the gap.
