What is this?

The dollar rate is the price of one US dollar in Brazilian reais. When the number goes up, the real is weaker: imports, travel abroad and fuel get more expensive, while Brazilian exporters (soy, oil, iron ore) earn more reais for the same dollars.

The figure shown is the PTAX sale rate, the official reference rate calculated by the Central Bank from the day's trades, and widely used in contracts. The chart averages each month to keep it readable; the headline numbers use the latest daily value.

What moves it: Brazil's interest rate vs. the US rate, commodity prices, the government's fiscal outlook, and global risk appetite.
