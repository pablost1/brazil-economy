What is this?

GDP (Produto Interno Bruto) is the total value of all goods and services produced in Brazil in a period. It is the main scorecard of how big the economy is and how fast it is growing.

IBGE publishes it every quarter. The top chart compares each quarter with the one before (seasonally adjusted, so Christmas or harvest effects are removed); the second chart sums up growth over the latest four quarters, which smooths out noise. The deep drop in 2020 is the COVID-19 shock; the big dip around 2015–2016 is the severe recession.
